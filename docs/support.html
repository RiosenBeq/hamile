Frequently asked

How is my week calculated?

From your estimated due date (or the first day of your last period + 280 days; IVF transfers are adjusted for embryo age). If your scan gives a new date, update it in You → Pregnancy details.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There’s no account, and we can’t see or recover your data. To move to a new phone, use You → Privacy & your data → Export a backup, then Restore on the new phone. Photos aren’t included in the backup file.

How do I restore Marigold Plus?

Open You → Restore purchases (with the same Apple Account you bought with).

How do I cancel my subscription?

In iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Marigold, or You → Manage subscription in the app.

Which languages does Marigold support?

English, Türkçe, Español, Deutsch, Français and Português (Brasil). Change it any time in You → Language.

Is Marigold medical advice?

No. Marigold shares general information from public health guidance and links to its sources, but it doesn’t know your history and can’t replace your midwife or doctor. See the Terms of Use.

I’ve had a loss. How do I stop pregnancy updates?

We’re so sorry. Go to You → Pregnancy loss support and turn on “Pause pregnancy content”. All baby updates and pregnancy notifications stop right away. Your journal stays safe.
